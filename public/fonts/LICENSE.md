# Bundled fonts

Rajdhani (Indian Type Foundry) and League Gothic (The League of Moveable
Type) are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org). They are bundled locally (latin subset,
woff2) so OpenMarket keeps working fully offline instead of loading them
from a CDN. The fonts are not covered by this project's own license.
